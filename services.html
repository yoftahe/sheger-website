Our Services

What We Offer

End-to-end vehicle export solutions and forward-thinking technology services — designed to deliver value at every stage.

Core Service

Vehicle Import & Export

At the heart of our business is the procurement and export of quality vehicles from the UAE to international markets. We manage the entire import-export cycle with precision, ensuring every unit meets destination country requirements and arrives in perfect condition.

Our established relationships with UAE-based dealers, auction houses, and manufacturer representatives give us access to competitive pricing and a diverse range of inventory — from brand-new vehicles to carefully selected pre-owned units.

  • New and pre-owned vehicle procurement
  • GCC and international specification vehicles
  • Bulk ordering for dealers and fleet operators
  • Competitive pricing through direct sourcing relationships

Vehicle Sourcing

Need a specific make, model, or configuration? Our sourcing team locates vehicles to your exact specifications. Whether it's a single unit or a fleet of 100, we leverage our extensive network across the UAE to find the right vehicles at the right price.

We provide detailed options for every sourcing request, including pricing comparisons, specification sheets, and availability timelines — so you can make informed decisions with full transparency.

  • Custom vehicle sourcing to exact specifications
  • Multiple quotation options per request
  • Access to auction, dealer, and manufacturer inventory
  • Fleet sourcing with volume pricing

Quality Assurance

Vehicle Inspection

Quality is non-negotiable. Every vehicle that passes through Sheger International undergoes a comprehensive multi-point inspection before it is cleared for export. Our inspection protocol covers mechanical systems, electrical components, body condition, and documentation verification.

For pre-owned vehicles, we provide detailed inspection reports with photographs, ensuring complete transparency for our clients before purchase confirmation.

  • Multi-point mechanical and electrical inspection
  • Body and paint condition assessment
  • VIN verification and documentation check
  • Detailed photographic inspection reports
Quality Inspection Process
Compliance

Export Documentation

Navigating international vehicle export regulations requires expertise and precision. Our documentation team handles every aspect of the paperwork process — from export permits and customs declarations to certificates of origin and bill of lading preparation.

We ensure full compliance with both UAE export regulations and the import requirements of every destination country, preventing delays and ensuring smooth customs clearance at the receiving port.

  • Export permit processing
  • Customs declaration and clearance
  • Certificate of origin preparation
  • Bill of lading and shipping documentation
  • Destination country compliance verification
Export Documentation
Logistics

Shipping Coordination

We coordinate end-to-end shipping logistics through our established network of freight partners. Whether your shipment moves via Roll-on/Roll-off (RoRo), containerized shipping, or air freight, we ensure competitive rates, optimal routing, and reliable delivery timelines.

Our logistics team monitors every shipment from Dubai to the destination port, providing real-time updates and proactive communication throughout the transit period.

  • RoRo, container, and air freight options
  • Competitive shipping rate negotiation
  • Real-time shipment tracking
  • Insurance coordination for transit protection
  • Delivery to ports across Africa and the Middle East
