Vehicle Import & Export

At the heart of our business is the procurement and export of quality vehicles from the UAE to international markets. We manage the entire import-export cycle with precision and discipline — ensuring every unit meets destination country requirements and arrives in perfect condition with complete documentation.

Our established relationships with UAE-based dealers, auction houses, and manufacturer representatives give us access to competitive pricing and a diverse range of inventory — from brand-new GCC-specification vehicles to carefully selected pre-owned units with verified service histories.

  • New and pre-owned vehicle procurement for all budgets
  • GCC and international specification vehicles available
  • Bulk ordering for dealers, fleet operators, and government bodies
  • Competitive pricing through direct sourcing relationships
  • Full transaction transparency from quote to delivery

Quality Assurance

Vehicle Inspection

Quality is non-negotiable at Sheger International. Every vehicle that passes through our pipeline undergoes a rigorous multi-point inspection before it is cleared for export. Our inspection protocol covers all mechanical systems, electrical components, body and paint condition, interior integrity, and documentation verification — nothing is overlooked.

For pre-owned vehicles, we provide comprehensive inspection reports complete with high-resolution photographs, clearly documenting the condition of every assessed element. Clients receive full inspection packages before purchase confirmation — there are no surprises.

  • Multi-point mechanical and electrical system inspection
  • Body panel, paint, and glass condition assessment
  • Interior condition, trim, and feature verification
  • VIN verification and documentation authenticity check
  • Detailed photographic inspection reports provided to client
  • Road test and functional systems evaluation

Compliance

Export Documentation

Navigating international vehicle export regulations requires specialist expertise and meticulous attention to detail. Our documentation team manages every aspect of the paperwork process — from UAE export permits and customs declarations to certificates of origin, commercial invoices, and bill of lading preparation.

We maintain current knowledge of the import requirements for every destination country in our network, ensuring full compliance at both origin and destination. This eliminates customs delays, prevents costly errors, and guarantees smooth port clearance for every shipment.

  • UAE export permit processing and approval management
  • Customs declaration and clearance at Dubai ports
  • Certificate of origin and commercial invoice preparation
  • Bill of lading coordination with freight partners
  • Destination-country import compliance verification
  • Complete document packages delivered to client in advance

Logistics

Shipping & Logistics Coordination

We coordinate complete end-to-end shipping logistics through our established network of international freight partners. Whether your shipment moves via Roll-on/Roll-off (RoRo), containerized shipping, or air freight, we secure competitive rates, optimize routing, and maintain reliable delivery timelines to every destination we serve.

Our logistics team actively monitors every shipment from the moment it departs Dubai to final arrival at the destination port — providing proactive status updates and handling any transit issues promptly and professionally.

  • RoRo, full container, and shared container shipping options
  • Air freight for urgent and high-value vehicle shipments
  • Competitive shipping rate negotiation on client's behalf
  • Real-time shipment tracking and proactive status updates
  • Marine insurance coordination for transit protection
  • Delivery to ports across Africa and the Middle East
