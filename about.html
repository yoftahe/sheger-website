Company Overview

Redefining Vehicle Export from the UAE

Sheger International LLC is a Dubai-based vehicle trading and export company, registered and operating within one of the UAE's premier free trade zones. The company operates at the intersection of automotive sourcing, quality assurance, and international logistics — delivering a seamless, end-to-end export experience to clients across Africa and the Middle East.

Our operations are structured to meet the demands of a rapidly evolving global automotive market. From individual buyers seeking a single vehicle to fleet operators and dealerships requiring bulk procurement, Sheger International provides comprehensive solutions that prioritize transparency, efficiency, and long-term value.

Executive Summary

Sheger International LLC was founded with a clear mandate: to bridge the gap between the high-quality vehicle inventory available in the UAE and the growing, underserved demand across emerging markets. Leveraging Dubai's position as a global trade and logistics hub, we offer a streamlined procurement and export pipeline that eliminates complexity and risk for our clients.

Our leadership team combines deep automotive industry knowledge with proven expertise in cross-border trade regulations, logistics coordination, and regional market intelligence. This positions us as a reliable, accountable, and efficient partner for vehicle buyers who demand both quality and results.

Our Mission

To deliver premium vehicles and comprehensive export solutions that consistently exceed client expectations — built on a foundation of trust, operational excellence, and an unwavering commitment to the highest industry standards.

Business Objectives

Our strategic objectives are designed to support sustainable, disciplined growth while maintaining the service quality that defines our brand:

  • Establish Sheger International as the preferred vehicle export partner for buyers across Africa and the Middle East through consistent performance and client-first service
  • Expand our sourcing network to include manufacturer-direct relationships, fleet allocations, and exclusive dealer partnerships across the UAE
  • Build long-term client relationships grounded in transparent pricing, reliable delivery timelines, and responsive after-sale support
  • Diversify service offerings into EV infrastructure, digital inventory platforms, and technology-driven business optimization solutions
  • Grow export volumes while maintaining a 100% compliance and documentation accuracy record across all destination markets

Company Description

Strategically Positioned for Global Trade

Operating from within one of Dubai's designated free trade zones, Sheger International LLC benefits from a business-friendly regulatory environment, world-class port infrastructure, and direct access to one of the world's most active vehicle re-export markets. The UAE processes millions of vehicle transactions annually — and our position at the centre of this ecosystem gives our clients a decisive advantage.

The UAE's free zone framework provides key operational advantages including 100% foreign ownership, streamlined customs procedures, and a tax-efficient operating structure — allowing us to pass cost efficiencies directly to our clients while maintaining full regulatory compliance.

Our Dubai headquarters serves as the central hub for vehicle procurement, quality inspection, documentation processing, and shipping coordination. Every aspect of our operation is engineered for speed, accuracy, and total transparency — from the initial client inquiry through to final delivery confirmation at the destination port.
