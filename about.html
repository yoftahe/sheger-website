About Us

Our Story

A Dubai-based vehicle export company built on integrity, precision, and an uncompromising commitment to quality.

Company Overview

Redefining Vehicle Export from the UAE

Sheger International LLC is a Dubai-based vehicle trading and export company, established within one of the UAE's premier free trade zones. The company operates at the intersection of automotive sourcing, quality assurance, and international logistics — delivering a seamless export experience to clients across Africa and the Middle East.

Our operations are structured to meet the demands of a rapidly evolving global automotive market. From individual buyers seeking a single vehicle to fleet operators and dealerships requiring bulk procurement, Sheger International provides end-to-end solutions that prioritize transparency, efficiency, and value.

Executive Summary

Sheger International LLC was founded with a clear mandate: to bridge the gap between high-quality vehicle inventory available in the UAE and the growing demand across emerging markets. Leveraging Dubai's position as a global trade hub, we offer a streamlined procurement and export pipeline that eliminates complexity for our clients.

Our team combines deep automotive industry knowledge with proven expertise in cross-border trade regulations, logistics coordination, and market intelligence. This positions us as a reliable and efficient partner for vehicle buyers who demand both quality and accountability.

Our Mission

To deliver premium vehicles and comprehensive export solutions that exceed client expectations — built on a foundation of trust, operational excellence, and an unwavering commitment to the highest industry standards.

Business Objectives

Our strategic objectives are designed to support sustainable growth while maintaining the service quality that defines our brand:

  • Establish Sheger International as the preferred vehicle export partner for buyers across Africa and the Middle East
  • Expand our sourcing network to include manufacturer-direct relationships and exclusive fleet allocations
  • Build long-term client relationships through transparent pricing, reliable delivery timelines, and exceptional after-sale support
  • Diversify service offerings into EV infrastructure and technology-driven business solutions

Company Description

Strategically Positioned for Global Trade

Operating from within one of Dubai's designated free trade zones, Sheger International LLC benefits from a business-friendly regulatory environment, world-class port infrastructure, and direct access to one of the world's largest vehicle re-export markets.

The UAE's free zone framework provides us with key operational advantages — including 100% foreign ownership, streamlined customs procedures, and zero corporate tax — allowing us to pass cost efficiencies directly to our clients while maintaining the highest compliance standards.

Our Dubai headquarters serves as the central hub for vehicle procurement, quality inspection, documentation processing, and shipping coordination. Every aspect of our operation is designed for speed, accuracy, and complete transparency from initial inquiry to final delivery at the destination port.
