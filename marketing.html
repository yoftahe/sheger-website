How We Work

Marketing & Sales Approach

We don't chase transactions — we build partnerships. Our approach to business is rooted in understanding our clients' operations, earning their trust, and delivering outcomes that create genuine, long-term value.

Our Philosophy

Relationships Before Revenue

In an industry where many operators compete on price alone, Sheger International competes on trust, consistency, and service quality. Our philosophy is simple: if we take care of our clients' businesses, our business takes care of itself.

01

Relationship-Driven Strategy

Every business relationship we build begins with understanding — not selling. We invest the time to learn each client's specific requirements, market position, buyer base, and growth objectives before making a single recommendation.

This depth of engagement has produced a client base built on genuine loyalty rather than contractual obligation. Our repeat business and referral network stand as the clearest evidence that the relationships we build are real and the value we deliver is consistent.

02

Deep Market Knowledge

Our team maintains an active, real-time understanding of both the UAE vehicle market and the demand characteristics of our destination markets. This dual perspective is a structural advantage — it allows us to source vehicles that aren't just available, but strategically aligned with what buyers in Africa and the Middle East actually need and can absorb.

We track pricing movements, inventory availability, model transitions, specification preferences, and regulatory shifts across every market we serve. This intelligence directly informs our sourcing decisions and gives our clients a competitive edge in their own markets.

03

Client-Focused Solutions

No two clients are the same, and our service reflects that reality. Whether you are a small dealer importing five vehicles per quarter or a large fleet operator requiring hundreds of units annually, we scale our offering to match your operation — not the other way around.

From flexible custom sourcing and multi-option quotations to priority shipping arrangements and dedicated account management, every element of our service is designed to make your procurement process simpler, faster, and more reliable.

04

Long-Term Partnerships

We measure success not by the number of transactions processed, but by the depth, longevity, and quality of our client relationships. Our goal is to become an embedded part of your supply chain — a partner your business genuinely depends on for consistent quality, predictable pricing, and reliable delivery.

We support our long-term partners with preferential pricing on volume orders, early access to high-demand inventory, market intelligence briefings, and priority response on all inquiries. When your business grows, we grow with it.

Our Process

How We Work with You

A clear, transparent process from first contact to final delivery — with no ambiguity, no hidden steps, and no surprises.

1. Initial Consultation

We begin with a focused conversation to understand your vehicle requirements, target market, budget parameters, preferred specifications, and timeline — building a complete picture of what you need before we act.

2. Vehicle Sourcing

Our procurement team identifies matching vehicles across our network, presenting detailed options with full specifications, pricing, availability timelines, and condition information for your informed selection.

3. Inspection & Approval

Selected vehicles undergo our full multi-point inspection protocol. We share comprehensive inspection reports and photographs for your review — your approval and confidence come before any purchase is confirmed.

4. Documentation

Our documentation team prepares the complete export paperwork package — export permits, customs declarations, certificate of origin, bill of lading, and any destination-country specific compliance documentation.

5. Shipping & Tracking

We coordinate shipment via your preferred freight method — RoRo, container, or air — with active monitoring and proactive status updates from Dubai departure through to destination port arrival.

6. Delivery & Aftercare

Upon arrival, we confirm documentation is complete for smooth port clearance and remain available for post-delivery support. The completion of one order is the beginning of the next relationship milestone.
