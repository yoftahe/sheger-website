What We Do

Comprehensive Export Solutions

From vehicle sourcing to final port delivery, we manage every step of the international export process with precision and full accountability.

Vehicle Sourcing

We procure vehicles to your exact specifications from UAE's extensive dealer networks, manufacturer allocations, and auction channels.

Quality Inspection

Every vehicle undergoes a comprehensive mechanical, electrical, and cosmetic inspection before export clearance is granted.

Export Documentation

Complete handling of export permits, customs clearance, certificates of origin, and all destination-country compliance paperwork.

Global Shipping

Coordinated logistics via RoRo, container, and air freight to ports across Africa and the Middle East, with full tracking.

EV & Charging Solutions

Cutting-edge electric vehicle supply and charging station infrastructure solutions for emerging and growth-stage EV markets.

Software Solutions
