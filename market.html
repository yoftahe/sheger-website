Africa

A continent of expanding opportunity with rapidly growing demand for reliable, durable, and value-driven vehicles.

Africa represents one of the largest and most dynamic vehicle import markets in the world. From commercial fleet operators in East Africa to government procurement bodies and individual dealers in West and Southern Africa, the demand for quality, durability, and proven performance drives every purchasing decision. Sheger International is strategically positioned — through both our Dubai location and our team's first-hand knowledge of the continent — to serve this market with vehicles that perform in Africa's diverse and often demanding conditions.

Key Markets We Serve

  • Ethiopia — Deep local market knowledge and well-established buyer networks
  • Kenya — Strong demand for commercial 4x4 and pickup vehicles
  • Tanzania — Growing market for pickup trucks and mid-range passenger vehicles
  • Uganda — Increasing demand for quality, competitively priced imports
  • Djibouti — Strategic port gateway for East African distribution and transit
  • Nigeria — High-volume market for new and quality pre-owned vehicles
  • South Sudan — Consistent demand for rugged vehicles suited to challenging terrain
  • Mozambique — Expanding commercial and utility vehicle market

Our African Market Advantage

  • Direct shipping routes to major African ports via established freight partners
  • Detailed understanding of country-specific import regulations and duty structures
  • Vehicles selected and specified for African road conditions and climates
  • Established relationships with dealers, NGOs, and fleet buyers across the continent
  • Volume sourcing capabilities that deliver competitive pricing on bulk orders
  • Team members with direct personal experience in target African markets

Middle East

A mature, vehicle-dense automotive market with sophisticated demand for premium, high-specification vehicles.

The Middle East is one of the world's most vehicle-dense regions, with consistently strong demand for both premium luxury SUVs and durable commercial fleet vehicles. Operating from Dubai places Sheger International at the geographical and commercial epicentre of regional trade — enabling rapid procurement, competitive pricing, and swift fulfillment for buyers across the Gulf and the broader Middle Eastern market. Our proximity to inventory and our knowledge of GCC specifications give us structural advantages no remote exporter can replicate.

Key Markets We Serve

  • UAE — Home base with direct, immediate access to dealer and auction networks
  • Saudi Arabia — The GCC's largest vehicle market with diverse demand
  • Iraq — Sustained demand for rugged SUVs and commercial pickup trucks
  • Oman — Growing appetite for luxury, off-road, and dual-purpose vehicles
  • Yemen — Ongoing demand for reliable commercial and utility vehicles
  • Jordan — Well-established import market with consistent, measured demand

Our Middle Eastern Market Advantage

  • Dubai-based operations for zero-delay procurement and immediate access to stock
  • GCC-specification vehicles — the preferred standard across the region — readily available
  • Comprehensive understanding of regional buyer preferences, trim levels, and feature requirements
  • Existing relationships with dealers, government procurement agencies, and fleet operators
  • Rapid delivery timelines for intra-Gulf shipments — often within days
  • Full documentation expertise for GCC and non-GCC destination country requirements
