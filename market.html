Regional Focus

Where We Operate

Our deep understanding of regional markets, import regulations, and buyer preferences allows us to deliver vehicles that match the exact needs of each destination.

Africa

A continent of opportunity with rapidly growing demand for reliable, durable vehicles.

Africa represents one of the largest and most dynamic vehicle import markets in the world. From commercial fleet operators in East Africa to individual buyers in West Africa, the demand for quality, affordability, and durability drives every purchasing decision. Sheger International is strategically positioned to serve this market with vehicles that perform in the continent's diverse conditions.

Key Markets & Strengths

  • Ethiopia — Deep local market knowledge and established buyer networks
  • Kenya — Strong demand for commercial and 4x4 vehicles
  • Tanzania — Growing market for pickup trucks and passenger vehicles
  • Uganda — Increasing demand for quality pre-owned imports
  • Djibouti — Strategic port gateway for East African distribution
  • Nigeria — High-volume market for both new and pre-owned vehicles
  • South Sudan — Demand for rugged vehicles suited to challenging terrain
  • Mozambique — Expanding market for commercial and utility vehicles

Our African Market Advantage

  • Direct shipping routes to major African ports
  • Understanding of country-specific import regulations and duties
  • Vehicles selected for African road conditions and climate
  • Established relationships with dealers and fleet buyers across the continent
  • Competitive pricing through volume sourcing

Middle East

A mature automotive market with sophisticated demand for premium and luxury vehicles.

The Middle East is one of the world's most vehicle-dense regions, with strong demand for both luxury SUVs and commercial fleet vehicles. Operating from Dubai places Sheger International at the epicenter of regional trade, enabling rapid fulfillment and competitive pricing for buyers across the Gulf and broader Middle Eastern markets.

Key Markets & Strengths

  • UAE — Home base with direct access to dealer and auction networks
  • Saudi Arabia — Largest vehicle market in the GCC region
  • Iraq — High demand for durable SUVs and pickup trucks
  • Oman — Growing market for luxury and off-road vehicles
  • Yemen — Demand for reliable commercial and utility vehicles
  • Jordan — Established import market with steady demand

Our Middle Eastern Market Advantage

  • Dubai-based operations for zero-delay procurement
  • GCC-specification vehicles readily available
  • Understanding of regional buyer preferences and specifications
  • Existing relationships with dealers, government agencies, and fleet operators
  • Rapid delivery timelines within the Gulf region
